# Visual direction

Based on the supplied mood board. The upper-left earthy palette is deliberately excluded.

- White page, light gray surfaces (#F5F5F5), fine borders (#E7E7EB).
- Blue (#3D4DFF) for primary actions, selected answers and progress.
- Green (#31D887) and red (#ED4965) for correct/incorrect result accents, with accessible darker text and explicit labels.
- Plain sans-serif interface, serif question headings, generous whitespace.
- One centered question at a time. Large undistorted media, two equal answer buttons, compact confidence controls.
- No image collage, tilted cards, stickers, decorative gradients or heavy shadows.

Seven screens: introduction, three image questions, one audio question, one text question, results. The introductory consent checkbox and confidence controls remain.
